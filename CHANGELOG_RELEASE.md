## isiBlightfallTracker 0.6.0

Thanks for the feedback!

- New options page: *Esc > Options > AddOns > isiBlightfallTracker* (or `/ibt options`)
- Choose the cue sound: voice "Go! Go!", built-in game sounds, or sounds from your shared-media sound pack
- Choose the sound channel (default Master) and test it with the "Play sound" button
- "Go! Go!" is now about 11 dB louder
- Window: size, background transparency, border on/off, lock
- Language: automatic, English or Deutsch
- Texts written out in full, no abbreviations (the window is a bit wider)
- Text stays readable at high background transparency (outline + shadow)
- Resizing keeps the left edge of the window in place
- Real time left of Virulent Plague and Dread Plague on your target (also in combat and Mythic+)
- Fix: plague and Dark Transformation timers now count Necrotic Coil and Graveyard (Forbidden Knowledge) as extensions
