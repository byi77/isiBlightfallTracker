## isiBlightfallTracker 0.7.0

- Blightfall estimate updated for the hotfix of 2026-10-07: Blightfall now deals 100 % of the remaining plague damage (was 200 %)
- New option "Show only when Blightfall is ready" (also `/ibt ready on|off`): the window appears with Dark Transformation and hides when you cast Blightfall; can be combined with "Show only in combat"
- Boss fights: the "NOW" signal before the end of the Soul Reaper debuff now also fires when adds are up and you use Epidemic, as in the simulation rotation (up to 3 enemies or a boss in the pull)
