## isiBlightfallTracker 0.4.3

First public release.

- Expected Blightfall damage (single target and all engaged enemies)
- "NOW" signal and voice cue at the right moment to press Blightfall
- Dark Transformation and plague bars, Soul Reaper window (+20 % bonus)
- After each fight: plague erupt damage and an estimated real vs. predicted comparison
- Self-calibrating, self-testing, local log
- Built for the 12.x addon restrictions, safe in Mythic+ and raids
- English and German
